# Changelog

All notable changes to Sabi Quest will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-01-03

### Added
- **Complete Nigerian State Coverage**: All 37 states now available for exploration
  - Each state includes unique landmarks, descriptions, and fun facts
  - States organized into Heritage, Mind, and Spirit zones

- **Comprehensive Curriculum Modules** (16 states with full content):
  - **Abuja**: "The People's Court" - Civics & Government
  - **Lagos**: "Balogun Market Challenge" - Mathematics, "Yaba Logic Challenge" - Logic
  - **Kano**: "The Ancient Trade Routes" - History
  - **Edo**: "The Benin Bronzes" - Art & History
  - **Enugu**: "The Coal City Story" - Science
  - **Plateau**: "The Nok Civilization" - History
  - **Bauchi**: "Yankari Wildlife Safari" - Science
  - **Anambra**: "Onitsha Market Mathematics" - Business Math
  - **Ogun**: "Words of Wole Soyinka" - English
  - **Oyo**: "The Oyo Empire" - History
  - **Osun**: "The Sacred Grove" - Culture
  - **Rivers**: "Oil and Energy" - Science
  - **Cross River**: "Carnival and Conservation" - Culture
  - **Sokoto**: "The Sokoto Caliphate" - History
  - **Borno**: "The Kanem-Bornu Empire" - History
  - **Taraba**: "The Mambilla Plateau" - Geography

- **Sabi Codex Encyclopedia**:
  - Nigerian folklore and mythology
  - Historical events and figures
  - Famous Nigerians profiles
  - Cultural traditions and food

- **RPG Features**:
  - Chibi avatar customization (skin tones, hairstyles, outfits, accessories)
  - Interactive journey map of Nigeria
  - Cultural guides for each region
  - Artifact collection system
  - Quest system (main, side, and daily quests)
  - XP and cowrie shell currency

- **CI/CD Pipelines**:
  - Automated testing on pull requests
  - Cross-platform builds (macOS, Windows, Linux)
  - Automated release generation
  - Rust dependency caching

- **Technical Features**:
  - SQLite offline database
  - Sound effects and animations
  - Framer Motion transitions
  - Responsive UI design
